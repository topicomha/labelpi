    How it works
    • Positions and sizes are % of the label: left/top 0 is the top-left corner, width 100 is the whole length. Thicknesses are in mm.
    • Text shrinks to fit its box. "Max size" (mm) stops it growing; 0 = as big as fits.
    • {field:Item} - a box to type into when printing
    • {date:%d %b %Y} - today, e.g. 28 Sep 2026
    • {date+3d:%d %b} - in 3 days; also w weeks, m months, y years, and - for the past
    • {time:%H:%M} - the time now
    • Formats: %d day, %b month (Sep), %m month (09), %Y year, %y year (26), %a weekday (Mon)
